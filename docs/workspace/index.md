# Find your way around

Home is the starting point for your characters, recent work and connection setup. On a computer, the workspace sidebar stays beside the main area. On a phone, open the workspace navigation when you need another page, then return to the chat.

## Choose a mode

| Mode | What you work with | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Roleplay](roleplay.md) | A character-chat transcript with message actions and swipes | Scenes, narration and long-form roleplay |
| [Conversation](conversation.md) | Direct messages and group conversations, with their own histories | Casual messages, ongoing friendships and a messaging-app feel |
| [Meower](meower.md) | A private character timeline with posts and reactions | Seeing your cast interact in a social feed |
| [Story Mode](story-mode.md) | A continuous manuscript view of a chat | Reading and writing without ordinary message bubbles |

Conversation keeps its own histories, so switching there opens a separate conversation. Story Mode changes how the current chat is displayed.

## Where things live

**Characters** holds the cast. **Persona** describes who you are when you speak. **Connections** selects the model service. **Lorebooks** adds relevant world information to requests.

**Notebooks** stores your own writing and files. **Scratchpad** gives you a side discussion with an assistant. **Agents** adds configured helpers around replies, and **Mewmory** handles source-backed recall for saved Roleplay histories.

The **Fine-tuning** area contains controls such as model presets, sampling, formatting and regex scripts. A regex is a text-matching rule used to find or replace patterns. You can leave these controls alone while you learn the main chat.

**Settings** includes appearance, device behaviour, account controls and import tools. **Included tools** opens features that ship with Neconyan; **Extensions → Manage extensions** manages whether extensions are enabled.

## Tours and everyday navigation

Home offers **Replay First paws tour**. Several full-page tools also have a character-led Tour. Follow the highlighted controls yourself; the tour explains the next action while you keep control of the page.

On a phone, a sheet or panel may take the full screen. Use its Back or close control to return to the chat. Opening every panel at once is rarely helpful on a small display.
